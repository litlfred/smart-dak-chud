    WHO guidelines, recommendations and guidance

    The DAKs are intended to reflect health recommendations and content that has already been published in WHO guidelines and guidance documents. The health content and interventions for this DAK are based on the following WHO documents:

    Document Document
    Early Warning and Response System (EWARS) for Dengue Outbreaks Dengue: Guidelines for Diagnosis, Treatment, Prevention and Control
    Early Warning and Response System (EWARS) - Dengue Edition  

    Other, non-WHO, references used in this implementation guide are listed below:

    • WMO-No. 1131
    • WMO-No. 1060
    • Guide to the WMO Information System, Volume II. WMO Information System 2.0. WMO-No. 1061
    • Climate Data Management System Specifications. WMO-No. 1131
    • WMO-No. 1281
    • Guide to the WMO Information System, Volume II. WMO Information System 2.0. WMO-No. 1061f
